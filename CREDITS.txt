TEXTURES THAT ARE NOT OURS
- The wrist and ankle bands in Textures/Actors/Character/Overlays/SimpleBleedoutKO/ (Wrist_Strangled_F/M, Ankle_Strangled_F/M) are
  reshaped from the "Bruise - Strangled" neck mark of "Battle Wounds" by AltNameByeee (LoversLab,
  https://www.loverslab.com/topic/154719-battle-wounds/ - its textures install as "Collygon - Wounds"). Included with the
  author's permission on credit ("As long as proper credit is given, I don't mind at all."). These textures are not covered
  by SimpleBleedoutKO's own licence.
- Battle Wounds itself is NOT included: it is a requirement for the skin marks. Thank you, AltNameByeee.

IDEAS AND MODS WE WORK WITH
- "Knockout - Health and Stealth-Based KOs" (Nexus Mods, Skyrim SE mod 133432): the ragdoll knock-out technique and the
  bash / sneak / low-health knock-out rules that SimpleBleedoutKO's own code follows.
- Injury Engine by GoodProvider (https://github.com/GoodProvider/Injury_Engine, MIT): its wounds drive the skin marks.
- SkyrimNet (https://github.com/MinLL/SkyrimNet-GamePlugin): the AI layer the prompt pack talks to.
- PLANCK by FlyingParticle: the hit-bone reading for the head-only rule in Skyrim VR.
- RaceMenu by expired6978 (and RaceMenu VR): the overlay painting.
